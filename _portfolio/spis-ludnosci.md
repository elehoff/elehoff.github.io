In 1921 and 1931, the Second Polish Republic carried out censuses of its population. To support my inquiry into the Jewish past in Poland between the First and Second World Wars, I have applied GIS methods to identify and analyze patterns of development and Jewish economic activity. I am currently preparing to make these datasets available.

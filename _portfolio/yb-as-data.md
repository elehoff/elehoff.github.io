Hundreds of volumes were published in several languages by émigrés and survivors from Jewish Eastern Europe. These *yizker bikher* offer insights into prewar Jewish life, Holocaust memory, and migration patterns. I have given [talks](https://elehoff.github.io/talks/) about this project and I have an upcoming [article](https://elehoff.github.io/publications/) on use of computational methods to analyze the language and images in these volumes.

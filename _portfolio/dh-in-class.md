When teaching in the Jewish Civilization sequence, I developed a weekly assignment to encourage students to think chronologically and spatially as we studied Jewish history, culture, and thought across five centuries. The interactive [timeline](https://cdn.knightlab.com/libs/timeline3/latest/embed/index.html?source=1FEKTKWo-yx8si64tZTukM6UklCSPzldCCw7fUwvyuxc&font=Default&lang=en&initial_zoom=2&height=650)and [map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?mid=1Z2PHv6je1gUmmEH6qVaxivDGtAGe-T8&ll=48.03693204775124%2C11.691376095150634&z=5) brought together students' contributions from throughout the term to reveal connections and distances among the texts and figures we studied.
